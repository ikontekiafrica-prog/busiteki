Leadership & Vision

Meet the Visionary Behind Busteki

Busteki was born from a bold vision to empower African businesses and unlock human potential through technology. At its core is a leader who lives this mission daily.

Verified Founder Profile

Jastin John Maile

Founder & Business Technology Coach

Coordinator, IAA Arusha DOT Climate Ambassador SME Startup Mentor

Jastin John Maile is a seasoned business technology coach and the visionary founder of Busteki. He currently serves as Partnership and Outreach Coordinator, dedicated coach, and trainer at the Institute of Accountancy Arusha (IAA), where he mentors emerging startups and SMEs in achieving long-term business sustainability through modern digital innovation.

A passionate trainer of university students and youth entrepreneurs, Jastin specializes in instilling the practical entrepreneurial mindset required to thrive in the modern tech economy. His journey is rooted in a lifelong mission to help young innovators, dreamers, and founders scale impactful ideas into thriving commercial ventures.

His core strengths lie in inspiring digital growth, nurturing grassroots innovation, and aligning traditional business processes with modern technological frameworks. Through Busteki and extensive community engagements, he continues to be a trusted beacon of practical knowledge and transformation across East Africa.

In addition to his entrepreneurial leadership, Jastin serves as a Climate Ambassador under Digital Opportunity Trust (DOT), where he mentors and empowers youth climate champions across four African nations. His work in climate action emphasizes sustainability, tech-enabled green innovation, and youth leadership — promoting a more resilient future through grassroots education.
