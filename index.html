Jastin John Maile is a business technology coach and the visionary founder of Busteki. He currently serves as Partnership and Outreach Coordinator, dedicated coach and trainer at the Institute of Accountancy Arusha (IAA), where he mentors startups and SMEs in achieving long-term business sustainability through digital innovation.

A passionate trainer of young students and youth entrepreneurs, Jastin specializes in building the entrepreneurial mindset required to thrive in the modern tech economy. His journey is rooted in a mission to help young innovators, dreamers, and founders scale impactful ideas into thriving ventures.

His strengths lie in inspiring digital growth, nurturing innovation, and aligning business with modern technology. Through Busteki and his community efforts, he continues to be a beacon of practical knowledge, motivation, and transformation across East Africa.

In addition to his entrepreneurial leadership, Jastin also serves as a Climate Ambassador under Digital Opportunity Trust (DOT), where he mentors and empowers youth climate champions across four African countries. His work in climate action emphasizes sustainability, innovation, and youth leadership — promoting a greener, more resilient future through education and grassroots impact.
